Honest by mechanism

Rules the scoring engine enforces, not promises in a slogan.

Policy · how eVal stays honest

  • A category nothing could check is hatched and written Not assessed. It is never shown as 100.
  • One Critical finding sets the verdict. An average cannot hide it.
  • MissingAn analyzer that is not installed or not reachable is reported, never silently skipped.
  • AIAI explanations are labelled, redacted on the way in, and never change a score.
  • AcceptAccepting a risk needs a reason, an owner and a review date. It reopens on that date.
  • ReadYour code is read, never executed. Scoring rules are deterministic and documented.

Note: Scores are risk indicators, not guarantees of production readiness.
